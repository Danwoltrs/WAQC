# Specialty Cupping - For Your Review and Approval

**Date:** 2026-06-01

**In one line:** A brand-new tasting screen that lets our cuppers grade top specialty coffee on the newest worldwide standard, quickly and beautifully, and hands back a premium coffee profile and certificate our clients will be proud to show.

---

## What this is

Today our cuppers grade everything the same way: a dense grid of numbers, fast and efficient for routine volume. That stays exactly as it is.

This is a second, separate screen built only for our finest specialty lots. It is modeled on the new global scoring standard that the specialty coffee world adopted in 2024, the one that officially retires the old 100-point sheet we all grew up on. Think of the difference between filling in a paper score sheet and being walked through a guided tasting that does the math, writes the notes, and prepares the certificate for you.

Routine coffee keeps flying through the quick grid we already use. Special lots get this richer experience. The two live side by side.

## Why it matters to the business

- **One language across every lab.** Santos, Colombia, Guatemala and Peru all score on the same scale with the same math. An 87 in Buenaventura means exactly what an 87 means in Santos. Finally, fully comparable.

- **Cuppers describe the coffee just by talking.** They hold a button and speak, in Portuguese, Spanish or English, and the screen captures it. No fighting a keyboard with a cup in hand.
- **The screen writes the words for us.** From the cupper's scores and chosen flavors, it drafts clean, professional tasting notes, the kind of language our sales team would otherwise write by hand for every lot.
- **A premium, client-ready result.** Each sample ends in a polished coffee profile and a one-click certificate that looks like something a top roaster would proudly put in front of their own customers.
- **It puts Wolthers at the front of specialty.** We would be scoring on the newest global standard with a digital experience very few labs anywhere can match.

## What the cupper sees and does

It feels less like data entry and more like a guided tasting, one step at a time:

- **Set the roast.** A quick tap to record how the coffee was roasted.
- **Rate eight things on a simple "how good is it" scale.** For each of the eight qualities a cupper judges (fragrance, aroma, flavor, aftertaste, acidity, sweetness, mouthfeel and overall), they pick a spot on a row of colored blocks that run from red for poor, through neutral gray in the middle, to green for excellent. One tap and it moves on. If a cup changes as it cools, a second tap records that.
- **Describe the cup once.** Rather than describing each quality separately, the cupper sums up the whole cup in one place: pick flavors off a colorful flavor wheel, or hold the button and say "lemon, jasmine, a touch of honey," and the screen offers up the matching flavors to confirm.
- **Mark the cups.** Five cups appear as five simple icons; the cupper taps any that taste off or inconsistent.
- **See the result.** A satisfying final score and a full coffee profile appear at the end.

No sliders, no dragging, no spreadsheet. Just tap, talk, and go.

## The payoff at the end

The reward for finishing is not just a number. It is a polished coffee profile our cuppers and sales team can use right away:

- **The score,** counting up and settling into a clear quality band, with the sample details.
- **A flavor fingerprint:** the flavor wheel with only this coffee's actual flavors lit up and the rest dimmed. The whole cup at a glance.
- **The words,** grouped by aroma, flavor and feel, each with a small tasteful icon, a flower, a citrus, a cocoa bean.
- **A written highlight:** a short professional tasting note plus an elegant wine- or whiskey-style description, "Notes of jasmine and stone fruit, with hints of honey," split into Nose, Palate and Finish. Available in Portuguese, Spanish or English, and ready to drop straight onto an offer sheet.
- **A one-click certificate** that bundles the score, the fingerprint, the notes and the description into the finished document, using the same certificate and tracking-number setup we already rely on.
