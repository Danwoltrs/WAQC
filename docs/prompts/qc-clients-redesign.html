What changed & why

  1. QC tag means something now. It only shows on actual QC clients (green pill), so the eye can scan QC vs trading-only instantly. The redundant per-row "QC Client" badge is gone — replaced by the filter chips up top.
  2. No more dash graveyard. Empty address / email / pricing cells render as nothing (or a soft "No email on file"), instead of a column of "–" that the eye keeps tripping over.
  3. Pricing column is QC-only. Trading clients leave it blank; QC clients show the rate (or a quiet "+ Set pricing" nudge when unset).
  4. Specs as a signal, not a list. The row shows a muted count ("4 specs") — enough to scan, not enough to clutter. A QC client with none yet gets an amber-dot "No specs — assign" flag (a real setup gap you want to catch); trading-only clients show nothing. The actual codes live on the detail page where you'd act on them.
  5. The switch unifies the form. Your instinct, refined: a single "QC client" toggle progressively reveals the whole config block — keeping the form clean for the ~55 trading-only accounts.
